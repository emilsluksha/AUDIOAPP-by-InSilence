# Logo preparation

Source supplied by the project owner: Неоновая волна на синем стекле.png.
Edited with the built-in image_gen tool, background-extraction mode.
Final asset: keywave-logo.png (RGBA, genuine transparent outer background).
keywave.png and the multi-resolution keywave.ico are derived by scripts/make_assets.py.

Prompt:
Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: Windows application icon and sidebar logo. Remove ONLY the external background around the supplied rounded-square dark blue glass tile with its cyan/blue ribbon waveform. Preserve the exact waveform shape, tile silhouette, internal dark glass, original cyan/blue colors, lighting and design. Remove the surrounding navy scene and the floating floor reflection beneath the tile. Output a clean centered square PNG with genuinely transparent alpha outside the tile, not a checkerboard painted into pixels. Tile should occupy about 90% of canvas with small transparent padding. No extra text, no new objects, no redesign.
